Administrator Navigation

This is the navigation sidebar for Administrator role (admin@company.com)

Employee Pages (Basic Access)

  • Dashboard - View leave statistics and recent requests
  • Apply for Leave - Submit new leave requests
  • My Leaves - View all your leave requests and their status

Manager Pages (Extended Access)

  • Leave Approvals - Approve or reject leave requests
  • Team Members Leave - View leave balances for team members
  • Team Leave History - View all reviewed leave requests
  • Team Leaves Calendar - Overview of team leave schedules

Administrator Pages (Full System Access)

  • Employee Management - Create, modify, and delete employee accounts
  • Leave Type Management - Configure system leave types
  • Leave Entitlements - Manage employee leave allocations
  • Email Templates - Manage system email notification templates
  • Email Preview - Preview different email notification templates
  • Movement Register - Track employee movements and attendance

Administrator Role

Administrators have full system access including all employee and manager features, plus the ability to configure system settings, manage employees, define leave types, manage entitlements, and customize email templates.
