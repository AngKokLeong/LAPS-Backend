Manager Navigation

This is the navigation sidebar for Manager role (Sarah Manager - sarah@company.com)

Employee Pages (Basic Access)

  • Dashboard - View leave statistics and recent requests
  • Apply for Leave - Submit new leave requests
  • My Leaves - View all your leave requests and their status

Manager Pages (Additional Access)

  • Leave Approvals - Approve or reject team member leave requests
  • Team Members Leave - View leave balances for team members
  • Team Leave History - View all reviewed leave requests
  • Team Leaves Calendar - Overview of team leave schedules
  • Movement Register - Track employee movements and attendance

Manager Role

Managers have all employee features plus the ability to manage team leave requests, view team schedules, and track team member attendance.
